# NotebookLM Experiment Log

## Experiment 1: Summarisation

Feature Tested:
NotebookLM Summary Generation

Prompt:
Provide a concise summary of the uploaded ESG reporting documents, highlighting the key themes and differences between the reporting frameworks.

Observation:
NotebookLM accurately summarised the uploaded ESG reporting documents and identified major distinctions between the IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards and the GRI Standards. This functionality may help students quickly understand lengthy academic readings.

---

## Experiment 2: Question and Answer

Feature Tested:
Question-and-Answer Capability

Prompt:
What are the main differences between IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards and the GRI Standards, and how could a postgraduate student use this information when preparing an ESG reporting briefing?

Observation:
NotebookLM generated a structured comparison grounded in the uploaded documents and linked the response to an authentic academic task. This feature can support coursework preparation and research activities.

---

## Experiment 3: Flashcards

Feature Tested:
Flashcard Generation

Observation:
NotebookLM automatically generated revision flashcards from the uploaded ESG materials. The flashcards supported active recall and may assist students preparing for examinations.

---

Reflection:

Overall, NotebookLM demonstrated strong potential as an academic support tool. However, outputs should always be reviewed against the original sources to minimise the risks of inaccuracies, over-reliance and potential hallucinations.
